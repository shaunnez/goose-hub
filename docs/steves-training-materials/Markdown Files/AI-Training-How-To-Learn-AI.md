WEEK 1  |  STEP 1
Learn Machine English

Understanding how AI really thinks — and why it matters for your prompts
AI doesn't understand language — it predicts it
Text is broken into tokens (words or word-parts)
Tokens become multi-dimensional vectors (numerical representations)
Similar concepts cluster together in embedding space
The model predicts the most likely next token based on context

Key insight: Vague prompts produce vague guesses
Sharp prompts produce sharp answers
This is why prompt quality directly determines output quality

WEEK 3  |  STEP 5
Steer to Experts

Direct the model toward specific frameworks and domain knowledge
Generic prompts produce generic answers
Instead of: "How do I improve my marketing?"
Try: "Using the StoryBrand framework by Donald Miller, analyze my homepage copy"

Name specific frameworks, researchers, and methodologies
This anchors the model to established, high-quality knowledge

The more specific your references, the more expert the response
You are curating the AI's knowledge — direct it to the best sources

KEY TAKEAWAYS
What to Remember

AI is a thinking partner

Treat it as a collaborator, not a vending machine. The quality of your conversation determines the quality of the output.
Vague input produces vague output. Use AIM to structure every interaction with clarity and intention.

Debug yourself first
